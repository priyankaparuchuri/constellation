One minute a day.

  1. Step 1

    Pick a small habit

    Less phone before bed, a short workout, a glass of water. Something you can do every day.

  2. Step 2

    Check in once a day

    One button. You can only check in for today, so the sky stays honest.

  3. Step 3

    Watch the sky grow

    Each day lights a star. Every week ends with a larger one. Gaps stay visible, and the next star joins the line.

A gap you can see, not a zero.

Streak counters turn one missed day into a number that falls back to nothing. A constellation keeps everything you have lit and marks what you missed, so you can see both at once.

Questions.

What if I forget a day?

A missed day stays missed and the sky keeps going. You can only check in for today, which keeps the record honest.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything is stored in your browser on your device.

Can I use it on another device?

Your data lives in one browser. In the Data tab you can download a backup and restore it somewhere else.

Who sees my data?

Only you, until you choose to send your export to the researcher. Diary notes are never part of that export.

Can I just try it?

Yes. Choose "Just trying it" when you start. You can switch between both versions and move through days quickly.
